## Advisor angle
With rate-hike odds rising, advisors may tell clients that bond prices can fall when yields jump. They may also review how concentrated a portfolio is in a single big tech stock after Nvidia's drop.

## Term of the day
**Yield:** the annual return a bond pays, relative to its price. When yields rise, existing bonds' prices fall.

## Interview talking point
"This week showed how Fed rate-hike expectations can move bond yields and stock prices at once, which is why I'd want to understand both when advising clients."
