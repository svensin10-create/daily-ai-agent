## Term of the day: CPI (Consumer Price Index)
CPI tracks the average change in prices consumers pay for a basket of goods and services. The Fed watches it when deciding whether to raise or cut interest rates. "Core" inflation leaves out food and energy because they swing a lot.

## Interview talking point
"With the Fed leaning toward a December hike, today's CPI report matters because it shows whether inflation is cooling. That shapes how I'd talk with clients about their bond and cash holdings."
