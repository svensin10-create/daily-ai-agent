## Advisor angle
After a one-day tech drop and with rate hikes possible, advisors would likely spend client calls on concentration risk. Clients heavily invested in AI and tech stocks may find their portfolio is riskier than they thought. The advisor's job is to review each client's goals and time horizon and explain what happened, not to react to one day's move.

## Term of the day: Correction
A correction is a drop of 10% or more from a recent high. It is milder than a bear market (usually 20% or more). Corrections happen from time to time, and the word describes the size of the fall, not what comes next.

## Interview talking point
"This week showed how a theme like AI can concentrate risk in a portfolio, and how rate-hike expectations can hit the same stocks, so diversification and client conversations matter."
