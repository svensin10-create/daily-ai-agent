### Advisor Angle
When discussing financing or housing with clients, advisors should point out that an October pause by the Fed does not mean the rate-hiking cycle is over. With economists still projecting a December rate increase and housing flashing warning signs, advisors can help clients set realistic timelines for debt obligations, mortgages, and cash liquidity rather than anticipating near-term borrowing relief.

### Term of the Day
**Pricing Power:** The ability of a business to increase the prices of its goods or services without negatively impacting customer demand or its overall revenue.
