## Term of the day: Basis point
One hundredth of a percentage point. A "50 basis point" hike is half a percentage point; a "25 basis point" hike is a quarter point.

## Interview talking point
"Wall Street is debating whether the Fed hikes 25 or 50 basis points in December, so I'm watching how that shift in rate expectations moves bond yields and client portfolios."
