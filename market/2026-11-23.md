## Advisor angle
Rates are expected to rise while stocks swing by 5-6% in a day. An advisor would likely spend client calls on volatility and rate risk. That means explaining why a one-day surge doesn't remove the risk behind the earlier correction. It also means reviewing how clients' bond and cash holdings respond to higher rates.

## Term of the day: Correction
A correction is a drop of about 10% or more from a recent market high. Fortune's headline says the Nasdaq entered correction before today's rebound. Corrections are common and don't always mean a longer downturn.

## Interview talking point
"Today's one-day Nasdaq jump of about 6% came right after a correction, and the Fed is expected to raise rates in December. That mix is why advisors focus on keeping clients disciplined through volatility."
