### Advisor Angle
With the Federal Reserve raising its key interest rate instead of cutting it, advisors must realign client expectations away from lower borrowing costs. Advisors would discuss how higher-for-longer policy rates affect borrowing, fixed-income yields, and broad portfolio risk, especially for clients anticipating central bank easing.

### Term of the Day
**Rate Hike:** An action taken by a central bank to raise its target benchmark interest rate. This makes borrowing money more expensive across the economy, which is intended to cool demand and bring down elevated inflation.

### Interview Talking Point
"With the Fed enacting its first rate hike in three years amid persistent inflation signals, and BCG identifying financial services as the sector most exposed to AI, adapting client portfolios to tight monetary policy while integrating automation will be central to the wealth management industry."
