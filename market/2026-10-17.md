### Advisor Angle
In light of the $124 trillion Great Wealth Transfer and rising female earning power, an advisor must shift conversations from individual asset management to comprehensive family estate planning, ensuring they build direct relationships with spouses and heirs early so assets do not leave the firm when wealth changes hands.

### Term of the Day
**The Great Wealth Transfer**: The multi-decade economic shift in which older generations pass down accumulated assets—projected at tens of trillions of dollars—to surviving spouses, heirs, and beneficiaries.

### Interview Talking Point
"Between navigating market volatility in concentrated tech trades and preparing for the $124 trillion wealth transfer, I believe great wealth managers must balance disciplined asset allocation with multi-generational relationship management."
