### Advisor Angle

When clients see headlines about the Federal Reserve lifting interest rates for the first time in three years, they often worry about rising debt costs and equity volatility. An advisor would explain that rate hike cycles typically follow resilient economic indicators—such as strong jobs data—and help clients contextualize these moves within their existing financial plans rather than making reactive portfolio changes based on policy speculation.

### Term of the Day

**Buying the Dip:** The practice of purchasing an asset or security after its price has experienced a short-term decline, based on the expectation that the drop is temporary and the asset will rebound over time.
