## Advisor angle
If Fed hikes keep coming, clients will ask about their holdings. Rising rates and a stronger dollar mean an advisor must revisit bond exposure, cash yields and borrowing costs, and reassure clients who are rattled by headlines like Nvidia's.

## Term of the day: basis point
A basis point is one hundredth of a percentage point. A "half-point" hike is 50 basis points, so a rate moving from 4.00% to 4.50% moves 50 bps.
